# Sandra Camilo Creator Brand

The domain for Sandra Camilo's creator-led business, spanning editorial content, commercial collaborations, and digital products.

## Language

### Brand and roles

**Creator Brand**:
Sandra Camilo's business as a creator, unifying her editorial content, brand collaborations, and digital products under one identity.
_Avoid_: Personal website, portfolio site

**Audience Member**:
A person who consumes or follows Sandra's editorial content. An audience member may also become a customer or represent a brand partner.
_Avoid_: Visitor, user, follower

**Customer**:
A person who purchases a digital product from the creator brand through a commerce provider.
_Avoid_: User, buyer

**Brand Partner**:
A business or organization that enters into a brand collaboration with Sandra.
_Avoid_: Client, sponsor

### Content and collaborations

**Editorial Content**:
Self-directed storytelling that Sandra shares publicly about travel, lifestyle, or recipes, regardless of its channel or format.
_Avoid_: Organic content, post

**Brand Collaboration**:
An explicit agreement for Sandra and a brand partner to create or publish content, whether paid or exchanged in kind. Unsolicited mentions and independent press coverage are not brand collaborations.
_Avoid_: Sponsorship, client project

**Collaboration Content**:
Content created or published as a result of a brand collaboration. Its origin in an explicit agreement distinguishes it from editorial content.
_Avoid_: Editorial content, sponsored post

**Portfolio**:
A curated showcase referencing selected editorial content and collaboration content without changing how that work is classified.
_Avoid_: Gallery, body of work

### Products and commerce

**Digital Product**:
A digital resource created by the creator brand for sale, such as a travel guide or Lightroom preset. Its availability determines whether it can currently be purchased.
_Avoid_: Download, item

**Planned Product**:
A digital product that may be publicly previewed but is not yet available for purchase. It must not present an active checkout action.
_Avoid_: Available product, purchasable product

**Product Catalog**:
The complete presentation of the creator brand's digital products, including planned products. “Products” is the preferred public label until purchasing is available.
_Avoid_: Guides, shop

**Travel Guide**:
A digital product containing curated destination knowledge, practical advice, and itineraries.
_Avoid_: Travel article, destination post

**Lightroom Preset**:
A digital product containing reusable Adobe Lightroom photo-editing settings.
_Avoid_: Filter

**Bundle**:
A single purchasable offer containing multiple digital products.
_Avoid_: Collection, package

**Commerce Provider**:
An external service responsible for checkout, payment processing, and digital delivery on behalf of the creator brand.
_Avoid_: Shop, payment gateway

### Communication

**Inquiry**:
A message sent to the creator brand as a collaboration inquiry, press inquiry, or general inquiry.
_Avoid_: Contact, request

**Collaboration Inquiry**:
An inquiry proposing a potential brand collaboration.
_Avoid_: Collaboration, lead

**Press Inquiry**:
An inquiry about an interview, feature, or other media request.
_Avoid_: Collaboration inquiry

**General Inquiry**:
An inquiry that is neither a collaboration inquiry nor a press inquiry, including travel questions and personal messages.
_Avoid_: Other, uncategorized inquiry
